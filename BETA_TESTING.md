# PHEISIRAETHA v0.1 Beta 1 — first-user guide

This is a limited public beta. Try one small, real intention that you can follow over time.

## Your first cycle

1. [Open PHEISIRAETHA](https://ugreat653-cyber.github.io/pheisiraetha-app/) and install it using the [installation guide](README.md#install-or-use-in-a-browser). You can also use it directly in the browser.
2. Complete the four onboarding pages on your first launch. They should not appear again after you finish them in the same browser/PWA.
3. Choose **Create intention** and describe one real intention. Use something small and meaningful that you can track, rather than a meaningless test goal. RIS is the saved definition of what you intend: the objective, success criteria, scope, non-goals, constraints and rationale.
4. Use **New check-in** to record what you intend now, your effort and feelings, and what you can actually observe. An intention describes what you want; an observed outcome describes what has happened so far.
5. Notice whether the questions are clear, whether the distinction between intention and observed outcome makes sense, whether your data stays saved, and whether changing language is convenient. Try a language change and check that your entered text remains intact.
6. Review **Intentional Revision** and **History**. Revision records a deliberate change to your intention; a changed outcome alone does not mean you have chosen a new intention. Check whether History makes the progression easy to follow.
7. Close the app and open it again. Check that your saved intention and completed check-in are still present and that completed onboarding does not repeat.
8. If the record matters to you, use **Data → Export backup (JSON)** and keep the file somewhere safe.
9. Report a problem or share feedback through the app's feedback link or [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/ugreat653-cyber/pheisiraetha-app/issues). The beta feedback template asks for device details and a short description; screenshots are optional.

## Feedback questions

- Was anything unclear during the first launch?
- Did you understand what RIS represents?
- Did any question feel ambiguous?
- Did you ever feel unsure what to do next?
- Did your entered text survive language switching and reopening?
- Did you understand the difference between changing an intention deliberately and simply recording an outcome?
- Did History make the progression understandable?
- Did anything look broken on your device?
- What would make you stop using the app?
- What was the most useful part?

Answer the questions that are useful to your experience; you do not need to complete a long survey.

## Your data and public feedback

Current v0.1 Beta stores entries locally in the browser/PWA on the current device. There is no account or cloud synchronization. Your record does not automatically move to another browser or device.

Deleting site/app data may permanently remove locally stored PHEISIRAETHA data unless you have exported a backup. Export JSON backups if your entries matter to you, and keep those files private.

**Do not include sensitive personal information in public GitHub Issues.** Do not publish your real intention, RIS/CIE entries or a private JSON backup. If a form problem depends on its content, describe the problem without sharing the real text, or use a harmless example. Remove private details from any optional screenshot.
